Privacy policy

No Human Onboard · last updated 20 October 2026

What this app is

"nohumanonboard" is a private tool used by the No Human Onboard channel to manage its own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page through the official Google and Meta APIs. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

What data it accesses

  • The channel's own YouTube data: videos, uploads, comments, and YouTube Analytics reports for the channel.
  • The channel's own Instagram account and Facebook Page: posts, comments and insights.
  • Public information about other public YouTube videos (titles, descriptions, view counts) for research.

It does not access anyone else's account, private data, contacts or messages.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the channel's content, read its performance and reply to comments on its own posts. Access tokens and analytics are stored locally on the channel operator's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in Meta Business settings. Locally stored data is deleted on request.
